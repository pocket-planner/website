Pocket Planner

A minimal desktop app for weekly task planning and daily workday check-ins. Keep your focus sharp and your plans entirely local.

Download for Windows (.exe)
Download for macOS (.dmg)
Or via Homebrew: brew install --cask pocket-planner

Weekly Planner Grid

A bird's-eye view of your entire week. Easily visualize, organize, and drag-and-drop tasks to keep your priorities in line.

Minimalist Power

Local-First & Private

Your plans are saved directly to your local file system via secure storage. No cloud databases, no tracking, total control.

Smart Workday Reminders

Configure notifications to gently nudge you with your "Next Up" task, active only during your specified workday hours.

OS System Integration

Control the app from the system tray, monitor task completion in the Windows taskbar, and view remaining count on the macOS Dock.

Weekly Carry-Forward

Start each week fresh. Review and carry forward incomplete tasks from the previous week with a single click to stay on top of your goals.
